Title: First Nepenthes ‘Gaya’ terrarium: what would you measure before adding fogger control?

I'm building a first terrarium around a pitcher plant labelled Nepenthes ‘Gaya’ and want to understand the environment before automating it.

I have the terrarium, plant, a 6 W LED, an ultrasonic fogger and a standalone thermometer/hygrometer. I'm planning low slate terraces, sphagnum with perlite, and a planting hollow in wood with drainage. The fogger will be hidden behind removable bark, with access for refilling. There will be no waterfall or stream.

I do not yet have a verified sensor time series or an automatic controller. I also haven't measured light at the leaves or confirmed the final enclosure dimensions. I don't want to infer that a fog-filled enclosure means the root zone is adequately watered.

For those who grow Nepenthes in terrariums:

- Where have you found humidity/temperature readings most useful, and where are they misleading?
- What observations tell you to reduce fogging or improve air exchange?
- What would you record during a manual baseline before deciding that automatic fogging is helpful?

This is an early hobby/learning project, not a product offer. I would value actual growing experience and criticism of the plan. The presentation images are AI concept illustrations, not photos of my current build.
